Salesforce Transfer Review

Load the review package to begin. Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

How this works

  1. Click Open review package and choose the review-package.json file you were given.
  2. Work through the items marked Needs a decision. For each one, edit anything that's wrong (text, type, date, tagged communities, sensors, and contacts), then choose:
    • Import: bring it over as shown (one record, tagged with everything listed).
    • Keep separate: for merged items, bring each Salesforce copy over on its own instead.
    • Skip: don't bring it over (for example, it's already in the app).
  3. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back.
  4. When you're done, click Save decisions file and send decisions.json back to the person running the transfer (secure share, not email).

Items marked Ready will be imported as shown unless you change or skip them. You don't have to look at them.
